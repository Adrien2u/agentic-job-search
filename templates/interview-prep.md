## Compensation handling

If it comes up early: {{DEFLECTION_OR_NUMBER}}

Deflection that works: *"I'd like to learn more about the scope before putting a number on it — do you have a range budgeted for the role?"*

If pressed, give a range, not a point, with your floor at the bottom: {{RANGE}}

_Several US states and jurisdictions require employers to disclose a range on request. Asking is normal and costs nothing._

## Don't do this

| Avoid | Instead |
|---|---|
| Criticizing a current or former employer | describe the situation neutrally, focus on what you want next |
| "I don't have experience with that" | "Here's the closest thing I've done, and here's how I'd get up to speed" |
| Rambling past 2 minutes | land the point, then stop and let them steer |
| Saying "we" for your own work | say "I" — they're evaluating you |
| Inventing a number | "I don't have that figure in front of me, but the direction was X" |
| Answering a question you weren't asked | answer the one asked |
