## Rules

1. **Two attempts on a cold contact.** Then stop. See `reference/cadence.md`.
2. **A warm introduction gets one follow-up**, then it goes back to the introducer. Chasing past that spends their relationship, not yours.
3. **Never contact two people at the same org about the same role** without saying so. It gets back to them and it reads badly.
4. **If `{{EMPLOYMENT_STATUS}}` is `employed`:** personal email, personal device, and think before messaging anyone connected to your current employer.
5. **Ask for something specific and small.** "Would you be open to 15 minutes about what the team's actually working on?" converts. "Can you help me find a job?" does not.
6. **Log it the same day.** A message you don't log is a message you'll send twice.
