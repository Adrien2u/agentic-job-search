# STAR Stories — {{FULL_NAME}}

Prepared answers to behavioral questions, organized by competency. Write them once, reuse them everywhere.

**S**ituation — the context, in one or two sentences.
**T**ask — what you specifically had to do.
**A**ction — what *you* did. Not the team. You. This is the longest part.
**R**esult — what changed. A number if you have one; if not, the concrete outcome.

Two stories per competency. Aim for stories that can flex to more than one question — most good ones do.

**Rules:** every story is true. Every number is real. You were actually there and it actually happened. An invented story survives the first follow-up question and not the second.

## Coverage check

- [ ] At least 8 stories written
- [ ] At least 6 have a real number in the Result
- [ ] Every story is from the last 5–7 years, or is unusually strong
- [ ] Actions are singular — "I," not "we" — even where the work was collaborative
- [ ] The failure story names a real failure
- [ ] No two stories come from the same project
- [ ] Each can be told in 90 seconds out loud. Time one.
